# 🏰 Brave Fortress

A medieval castle defense game built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Train warriors, defend your kingdom, earn gold, upgrade your fortress and survive increasingly dangerous enemy waves.

Brave Fortress combines strategy, resource management and real-time combat into a simple but addictive browser game.

---

# 🎮 Features

- Medieval fantasy theme
- Castle defense gameplay
- Trainable units
- Enemy waves
- Boss battles
- Gold economy
- Fortress upgrades
- High score saving
- Particle effects
- Responsive design

# 👾 Enemies

Fight against various enemies:

- Goblins
- Orcs
- Trolls
- Ogre King Boss

---

# 🤖 Boss Waves

Every 5th wave introduces a boss enemy.

Bosses feature:

- Massive HP
- Increased damage
- Greater rewards
- Larger size

Defeat them to earn bonus gold and score.

---

# 💰 Economy System

Earn gold by:

- Defeating enemies
- Completing waves

Spend gold on:

- New units
- Fortress upgrades

---

## 🏰 Stronger Walls

---

## 🔥 Blacksmith

Improves damage of all units.

Starting Castle Health:


If the castle HP reaches zero:

