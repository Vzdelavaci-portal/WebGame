# 🏍️ Neon Tron

A fast-paced browser arcade game inspired by classic light-cycle battles.

Control your neon bike, leave a glowing trail behind you, collect Data Cores, use boost strategically and force your opponent to crash.

The first player to win **5 rounds** becomes the Neon Champion.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏍️ Light-cycle inspired gameplay
- 🤖 Player vs AI mode
- 👥 Local 2 Player mode
- 🎯 Three AI difficulty levels
- 🌈 Permanent neon trails
- ⚡ Boost system
- ◇ Data Cores
- 🟡 Temporary Phase Mode
- 💥 Particle explosions
- 📳 Screen shake effects
- 🏆 Round-based scoring
- ⏸ Pause & Restart
- 🎮 Keyboard controls
- 🚫 No external libraries required

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 🤖 Player vs AI

Challenge a computer-controlled neon bike.

Available difficulties:

- Easy
- Normal
- Hard

The AI analyzes free space and tries to avoid walls and trails.

---

### 👥 Two Players

Play locally against another player on the same keyboard.

Each player controls their own neon bike and boost.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Survive longer than your opponent.

Every bike leaves a permanent light trail behind it.

You lose the round if you crash into:

- Arena walls
- Your own trail
- Your opponent's trail
