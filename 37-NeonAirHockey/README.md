# 🏒 Neon Air Hockey

A fast-paced browser air hockey game filled with glowing neon visuals, smooth physics and exciting arcade gameplay.

Challenge the AI, play against a friend and become the first player to score **7 goals**!

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏒 Player vs AI mode
- 👥 Local 2 Player mode
- 🤖 Three AI difficulty levels
- ⚡ Arcade-style puck physics
- 🌟 Neon glow effects
- 💫 Dynamic puck trail
- 💥 Particle explosions
- 📳 Screen shake on goals
- ⚡ Power Shot system
- 📊 Live score tracking
- ⏸ Pause & Restart
- 🖱 Mouse, keyboard and touch controls
- 📱 Responsive design
- 🚫 No external libraries

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 🤖 Player vs AI

Play against an intelligent computer opponent.

Available difficulties:

- Easy
- Normal
- Hard

---

### 👥 Two Players

Challenge your friend on the same keyboard.

Player 1 controls the bottom paddle.

Player 2 controls the top paddle.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Your mission is simple:

Score **7 goals** before your opponent.

Hit the puck, defend your goal and use powerful shots to surprise your rival.

---

## ⚡ Power Shot

Every successful hit charges your paddle.

Once the energy bar reaches **100%**, your next hit becomes a **Power Shot**.

Power Shot features:

- 🚀 Much faster puck
- 🌟 Yellow neon trail
- 💥 Bigger particle explosion
- 📳 Stronger screen shake
- ⚡ Extra exciting gameplay

---

## 🎮 Controls

### Player 1

| Key | Action |
|------|--------|
| Mouse | Move paddle |
| Touch | Move paddle |
| A | Move left |
| D | Move right |

---

### Player 2

| Key | Action |
|------|--------|
| ← | Move left |
| → | Move right |

---

### General

| Key | Action |
|------|--------|
| P | Pause |
| ESC | Pause |
| Enter | Restart after Game Over |

You can also use the **Pause** and **Restart** buttons.
