# 🐸 Neon Frogger

A modern neon-inspired remake of the classic Frogger arcade game built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Guide your glowing frog through busy highways, dangerous rivers, floating logs and turtles to reach all five safe homes. Every completed level increases the challenge with faster traffic and moving platforms.

---

# 🎮 Features

- 🌈 Neon cyberpunk visual style
- 🚗 Moving cars and trucks
- 🌊 Dangerous river with floating logs
- 🐢 Turtles that can disappear underwater
- 🏠 Five goal homes to complete each level
- ❤️ Multiple lives
- ⏱ Countdown timer
- 📈 Progressive difficulty
- ⭐ Power-ups
  - Shield
  - Slow Motion
  - Double Score
- ✨ Particle effects
- 🎮 Keyboard controls
- 📱 Mobile touch controls
- ⏸ Pause & Restart
- 💯 Score system
- 🚀 Responsive design

---

# 🎮 Controls

### Keyboard

- ⬆️ Up
- ⬇️ Down
- ⬅️ Left
- ➡️ Right

or

- **W A S D**

Additional controls

- **P** or **ESC** → Pause
- **Enter** → Restart after Game Over

---

# 🎯 Objective

Reach all **five homes** at the top of the map.

Avoid:

- 🚗 Cars
- 🚚 Trucks
- 🌊 Water

Ride safely on:

- 🪵 Logs
- 🐢 Turtles

Complete all homes to advance to the next level.

---

# ⭐ Power-ups

🛡 Shield
- Protects you from one collision.

⏱ Slow Motion
- Slows down all moving obstacles.

⭐ Double Score
- Doubles all earned points for a limited time.
